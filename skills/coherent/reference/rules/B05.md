# B05 — Helper proliferation

Investigation procedure only. Catalog metadata lives in
[taxonomy.md](../taxonomy.md). Detection: hybrid. No detector ships yet.
Record: `semantic-findings.json` until a candidate exists, then
`reviews.json`. Shared write-back: [README.md](README.md).

## What to search for

`utils/`, `helpers/`, `lib/misc`, `common/` folders; many one-function files
with no owner; domain rules living in `formatX` / `ensureY` / `toZ`.

Search for equivalent behavior before treating a helper as unique. After
A04/A06 consolidations, helpers often become the leftover junk drawer.

## Distinguish a real issue from an intentional difference

- Intentional: a small shared function with one owner module and a clear
  domain name.
- Real: domain behavior is scattered; later agents keep extending the
  drawer.
- Not B05: one forwarding wrapper (B04); a premature interface (B03).

Prefer deleting or moving helpers back to the owning concept over adding a
“better” utils package.

## Evidence to collect

- Helper symbols, owners (or lack of owners), and call sites.
- Which domain concept each helper secretly belongs to.
- Duplicates that A04 should have claimed.

## Successful “do not merge”

`fingerprintFinding` and `createFinding` are small and owned by `Finding`.
They are not a junk drawer. Leave them.

## How to write the result

Append `identity`: `helper-sprawl:<folder-or-concept>` to
`semantic-findings.json`, or review a mechanical finding if one appears.

# E02 — Repeated computation

Investigation procedure only. Catalog metadata lives in
[taxonomy.md](../taxonomy.md). Detection: hybrid. No detector ships yet.
Record: `decisions.json` until a candidate exists, then
`decisions.json`. Shared write-back: [README.md](README.md). Fix ownership
(A04/C01) first; then the leftover hot path.

## What to search for

Pure or expensive work recomputed in a loop or across layers: the same
map/filter/parse/hash, repeated schema validation, rebuilt indexes per
request.

## Distinguish a real issue from an intentional difference

- Intentional: recomputation because inputs changed or a write invalidated
  the result.
- Real: a local variable or owner would be correct.
- Not E02: N+1 queries (E01); nested `.find()` (E06).

## Evidence to collect

- Expression, call sites, and why inputs are unchanged.
- The owner that should keep the result.

## Successful “do not merge”

Two similar parses of different payloads. Keep both.

## How to write the result

Append `identity`: `repeated-compute:<file>:<fn>` or review a mechanical
finding. Confirm only on a surviving hot path.
